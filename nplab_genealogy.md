# Genealogy of NPLab Ideas

How the lab came to think the way it does — one landmark per member, from the first
persistent-homology papers (2013) through higher-order networks to Bound by
Semanticity. Read in sequence, it shows where the lab's questions come from, and who
built each idea. Not a prerequisite.

## Reading

- **Petri, Scolamiero, Donato & Vaccarino (2013)** — *Topological strata of weighted complex networks.* PLOS ONE. First persistent homology on weighted networks. Early NPLab TDA. [doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0066506](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0066506)
- **Petri, Expert, Turkheimer, Carhart-Harris, Nutt, Hellyer & Vaccarino (2014)** — *Homological scaffolds of brain functional networks.* J. R. Soc. Interface. Introduced homological scaffolds. The lab's most-cited neuroscience paper. [doi:10.1098/rsif.2014.0873](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2014.0873)
- **Patania, Selvaggi, Veronese, Dipasquale, Expert & Petri (2019)** — *Topological gene expression networks recapitulate brain anatomy and function.* Network Neuroscience. Alice Patania — now Indiana University Bloomington. [doi:10.1162/netn_a_00094](https://doi.org/10.1162/netn_a_00094)
- **Iacopini, Petri, Barrat & Latora (2019)** — *Simplicial models of social contagion.* Nature Communications. Iacopo Iacopini — now Associate Professor at Northeastern University London. Launched higher-order contagion. [doi:10.1038/s41467-019-10431-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-10431-6)
- **Arnaudon, Peach, Petri & Expert (2022)** — *Connecting Hodge and Sakaguchi–Kuramoto through a mathematical framework for coupled oscillators on simplicial complexes.* Communications Physics. Alexis Arnaudon + Robert Peach. [doi:10.1038/s42005-022-00963-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-022-00963-7)
- **Lucas, Iacopini, Robiglio, Barrat & Petri (2023)** — *Simplicially driven simple contagion.* Physical Review Research. Maxime Lucas — now Namur/UCLouvain. [doi:10.1103/physrevresearch.5.013201](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevresearch.5.013201)
- **Mancastroppa, Iacopini, Petri & Barrat (2023)** — *Hyper-cores promote localization and efficient seeding in higher-order processes.* Nature Communications. Mattia Mancastroppa. [doi:10.1038/s41467-023-41887-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-41887-2)
- **Santoro, Battiston, Petri & Amico (2023)** — *Higher-order organization of multivariate time series.* Nature Physics. Andrea Santoro — current postdoc. [doi:10.1038/s41567-022-01852-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-022-01852-0)
- **Nurisso, Arnaudon, Lucas, Peach, Expert, Vaccarino & Petri (2024)** — *A unified framework for simplicial Kuramoto models.* Chaos. Marco Nurisso — current PhD. [doi:10.1063/5.0169388](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0169388)
- **Santoro, Battiston, Lucas, Petri & Amico (2024)** — *Higher-order connectomics of human brain function.* Nature Communications. Andrea Santoro. [doi:10.1038/s41467-024-54472-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-54472-y)
- **Nurisso, Morandini, Lucas, Vaccarino, Gili & Petri (2025)** — *Higher-order Laplacian renormalization.* Nature Physics. Marco Nurisso + Maxime Lucas. Most recent Nature Physics paper. [doi:10.1038/s41567-025-02784-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-025-02784-1)
- **Nurisso, Fernando et al. (2025)** — *Bound by Semanticity.* ICLR 2026. Marco Nurisso + Jesseba Fernando. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2506.14797](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2506.14797) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14797)
- **Savietto, Campbell, Panisson, Nurisso, Petri, Cohen & Perotti (2026)** — *The geometry of representational failures in vision language models.* ICML 2026 (accepted). arXiv:2602.07025 Daniele Savietto. The lab's first ICML paper; extends semanticity to synthetic agents. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2602.07025](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2602.07025) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07025)
- **Guadagnuolo, Nurisso, Galluzzi, Allard & Petri (2026)** — *Harmonic morphisms and dynamical invariants in network renormalization.* arXiv:2604.08386 Most recent renormalization work; submitted to PRX. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.08386](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.08386) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08386)
