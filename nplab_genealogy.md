# Genealogy of NPLab Ideas

How the lab came to think the way it does — one landmark per member, from the first
persistent-homology papers (2013) through higher-order networks to Bound by
Semanticity. Read in sequence, it shows where the lab's questions come from, and who
built each idea. Not a prerequisite.

## Reading

- **Petri, Scolamiero, Donato & Vaccarino (2013)** — *Topological strata of weighted complex networks.* PLOS ONE. First persistent homology on weighted networks. Early NPLab TDA.
- **Petri, Expert, Turkheimer, Carhart-Harris, Nutt, Hellyer & Vaccarino (2014)** — *Homological scaffolds of brain functional networks.* J. R. Soc. Interface. Introduced homological scaffolds. The lab's most-cited neuroscience paper.
- **Patania, Selvaggi, Veronese, Dipasquale, Expert & Petri (2019)** — *Topological gene expression networks recapitulate brain anatomy and function.* Network Neuroscience. Alice Patania — now Indiana University Bloomington.
- **Iacopini, Petri, Barrat & Latora (2019)** — *Simplicial models of social contagion.* Nature Communications. Iacopo Iacopini — now Associate Professor at Northeastern University London. Launched higher-order contagion.
- **Arnaudon, Peach, Petri & Expert (2022)** — *Connecting Hodge and Sakaguchi–Kuramoto through a mathematical framework for coupled oscillators on simplicial complexes.* Communications Physics. Alexis Arnaudon + Robert Peach.
- **Lucas, Iacopini, Robiglio, Barrat & Petri (2023)** — *Simplicially driven simple contagion.* Physical Review Research. Maxime Lucas — now Namur/UCLouvain.
- **Mancastroppa, Iacopini, Petri & Barrat (2023)** — *Hyper-cores promote localization and efficient seeding in higher-order processes.* Nature Communications. Mattia Mancastroppa.
- **Santoro, Battiston, Petri & Amico (2023)** — *Higher-order organization of multivariate time series.* Nature Physics. Andrea Santoro — current postdoc.
- **Nurisso, Vaccarino & Petri (2024)** — *A unified framework for simplicial Kuramoto.* Chaos. Marco Nurisso — current PhD.
- **Santoro, Battiston, Lucas, Petri & Amico (2024)** — *Higher-order connectomics of human brain function.* Nature Communications. Andrea Santoro.
- **Nurisso, Morandini, Lucas, Vaccarino, Gili & Petri (2025)** — *Higher-order Laplacian renormalization.* Nature Physics. Marco Nurisso + Maxime Lucas. Most recent Nature Physics paper.
- **Nurisso, Fernando et al. (2025)** — *Bound by Semanticity.* ICLR 2026. Marco Nurisso + Jesseba Fernando.
- **Savietto, Campbell, Panisson, Nurisso, Petri, Cohen & Perotti (2026)** — *The geometry of representational failures in VLMs.* ICML 2026 (accepted). arXiv:2602.07025 Daniele Savietto. The lab's first ICML paper; extends semanticity to synthetic agents.
- **Guadagnuolo, Nurisso, Galluzzi, Allard & Petri (2026)** — *Harmonic morphisms and dynamical invariants in network renormalization.* arXiv:2604.08386 Most recent renormalization work; submitted to PRX.
