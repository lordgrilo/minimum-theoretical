# Topology Beyond One Parameter

Single-parameter persistence is the Tier 1 working tool. Real data rarely come with
one natural filtration: density and scale, time and threshold, signal and noise pull
in different directions. Multiparameter persistence is where topology has to go to
handle that honestly, and its theory is still unsettled (no complete discrete
invariant exists). It is listed here as a frontier, not as a method ready for
routine use.

## Reading

- **Botnan & Lesnick (2023)** — *An introduction to multiparameter persistence.* arXiv:2203.14289 NEW: frontier TDA — filtrations along multiple parameters.
