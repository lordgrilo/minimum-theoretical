# Operator Algebras & Quantum Higher-Order Interactions (speculative — not part of the minimum)

**Speculative reading cluster — not part of the minimum.** A speculative direction:
the Fock-space structure of tensor-product representations, capacity limits as
algebraic invariants, and a quantum analogue of higher-order synergy. There is no
mature literature on quantum higher-order interactions yet; these readings are
foundations, not an established canon.

## Reading

- **Smolensky (1990)** — *Tensor product variable binding.* (See Tier 1: Foundational Papers.) The starting point: TPR and the 1/n collapse. [doi:10.1016/0004-3702(90)90007-M](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702%2890%2990007-M)
- **Penrose (2004)** — *The Road to Reality.* (chapters on Hilbert spaces, operator algebras, Fock spaces). Accessible mathematical background for the speculation. [link](https://books.google.com/books?vid=ISBN9780224044479)
- **Jansma (2023)** — *Higher-order interactions and their duals reveal synergy and logical dependence beyond Shannon information.* Entropy 25(4):648. arXiv:2205.04440 Möbius-inversion view of synergy; a real bridge toward algebraic HOI. [doi:10.3390/e25040648](https://doi.org/10.3390/e25040648)
- **Upmeier (1987)** — *Jordan Algebras in Analysis, Operator Theory, and Quantum Mechanics.* CBMS Regional Conference Series in Mathematics 67, AMS Non-associative algebra in QM; scaffolding for a quantum-synergy analogue. (Speculative.) [doi:10.1090/cbms/067](https://doi.org/10.1090/cbms/067)
