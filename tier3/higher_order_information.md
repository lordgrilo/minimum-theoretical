# Irreducible Multivariate Information

How to quantify genuine multivariate dependency — synergy, redundancy, integration —
that pairwise measures cannot see, and how to estimate it without fooling yourself.
This is the engine under our work on higher-order information in neural,
clinical and developmental data.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Rosas, Mediano, Gastpar & Jensen (2019)** — *Quantifying high-order interdependencies via multivariate extensions of the mutual information.* Physical Review E. Introduced the O-information (signed synergy/redundancy).
- ⭐ **Williams & Beer (2010)** — *Nonnegative decomposition of multivariate information.* arXiv:1004.2515. Foundational Partial Information Decomposition (PID).
- ⭐ **Timme & Lapish (2018)** — *A tutorial for information theory in neuroscience.* eNeuro. Practical guide to applying IT measures to neural data.
- **Varley & Sporns (2022)** — *Multivariate information theory.* (tutorial/review). Review of multivariate IT measures.
- **Robiglio, Neri, Iacopini, Petri & Barrat (2024)** — *Synergistic signatures of group mechanisms in higher-order systems.* Connects O-information to structural higher-order properties.
- **Combrisson, Allegra, Basanisi, Ince, Giordano, Bastin, Petri & Brovelli (2025)** — *Higher-order and distributed synergistic functional interactions encode information gain in goal-directed learning.* Nature Communications. Synergy encodes information gain in learning.
- **Kolchinsky (2022)** — *A novel approach to the partial information decomposition.* Entropy. Connects PID to the Information Bottleneck; clarifies axioms.
- **Mediano, Rosas, Luppi, Carhart-Harris, Bor, Seth & Barrett (2021)** — *Towards an extended taxonomy of information dynamics via Integrated Information Decomposition.* arXiv:2109.13186 Unifies integrated information and synergy under one framework (ΦID).
- **Stramaglia, Faes, Cortes & Marinazzo (2024)** — *Disentangling high-order effects in the transfer entropy.* Physical Review Research 6:L032007 Decomposes transfer entropy into unique/redundant/synergistic atoms (directed HOI).
- **Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi (2014)** — *From the phenomenology to the mechanisms of consciousness: IIT 3.0.* PLoS Computational Biology. Integrated information theory, practical formalism.
- **Albantakis et al. (2023)** — *Integrated Information Theory (IIT) 4.0.* PLoS Computational Biology. Latest IIT formulation; intrinsic information.
