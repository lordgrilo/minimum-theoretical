# Irreducible Multivariate Information

How to quantify genuine multivariate dependency — synergy, redundancy, integration —
that pairwise measures cannot see, and how to estimate it without fooling yourself.
These are the measures behind higher-order analyses of neural, clinical and
developmental data.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Rosas, Mediano, Gastpar & Jensen (2019)** — *Quantifying high-order interdependencies via multivariate extensions of the mutual information.* Physical Review E. Introduced the O-information (signed synergy/redundancy). [doi:10.1103/physreve.100.032305](https://doi.org/10.1103/physreve.100.032305)
- ⭐ **Williams & Beer (2010)** — *Nonnegative decomposition of multivariate information.* arXiv:1004.2515. Foundational Partial Information Decomposition (PID). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1004.2515](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1004.2515) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.2515)
- ⭐ **Timme & Lapish (2018)** — *A tutorial for information theory in neuroscience.* eNeuro. Practical guide to applying IT measures to neural data. [doi:10.1523/eneuro.0052-18.2018](https://doi.org/10.1523/eneuro.0052-18.2018)
- **Rosas, Mediano, Luppi, Boyd, Berta, Jensen, Gastpar & Carroll (2026)** — *Four faces of information in natural systems.* Nature Reviews Physics 8, 654–666. A current map of what information can mean in natural systems; useful background when choosing between decompositions. [doi:10.1038/s42254-026-00971-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-026-00971-4)
- **Dupret, Fusi & Panzeri (2026)** — *Neural population activity for memory: properties, computations, and codes.* Neuron 114, 390–407. Redundancy and synergy in memory codes. It does not equate redundancy with stability or synergy with flexibility; that mapping goes beyond the paper. [doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2025.11.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2025.11.007)
- **Antonacci, Barà, Sparacino, Marinazzo, Faes & Stramaglia (2026)** — *From statistical to structural synergy: a predictability framework to quantify the effects due to high-order mechanisms.* arXiv:2607.07286. Separates statistical synergy from synergy produced by genuine high-order mechanisms. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2607.07286](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2607.07286) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07286)
- **Varley (2025)** — *Information theory for complex systems scientists: what, why, and how.* Physics Reports 1148, 1–55. Review of multivariate IT measures. [doi:10.1016/j.physrep.2025.09.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2025.09.007)
- **Robiglio, Neri, Coppes, Agostinelli, Battiston, Lucas & Petri (2025)** — *Synergistic signatures of group mechanisms in higher-order systems.* Physical Review Letters 134, 137401. Connects O-information to structural higher-order properties. [doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.137401](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.137401)
- **Combrisson, Basanisi, Neri, Auzias, Petri, Marinazzo, Panzeri & Brovelli (2025)** — *Higher-order and distributed synergistic functional interactions encode information gain in goal-directed learning.* Nature Communications 16, 7179. Synergy encodes information gain in learning. [doi:10.1038/s41467-025-62507-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62507-1)
- **Kolchinsky (2022)** — *A novel approach to the partial information decomposition.* Entropy. Connects PID to the Information Bottleneck; clarifies axioms. [doi:10.3390/e24030403](https://doi.org/10.3390/e24030403)
- **Mediano, Rosas, Luppi, Carhart-Harris, Bor, Seth & Barrett (2021)** — *Towards an extended taxonomy of information dynamics via Integrated Information Decomposition.* arXiv:2109.13186 Unifies integrated information and synergy under one framework (ΦID). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2109.13186](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2109.13186) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.13186)
- **Stramaglia, Faes, Cortes & Marinazzo (2024)** — *Disentangling high-order effects in the transfer entropy.* Physical Review Research 6:L032007 Decomposes transfer entropy into unique/redundant/synergistic atoms (directed HOI). [doi:10.1103/physrevresearch.6.l032007](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevresearch.6.l032007)
- **Oizumi, Albantakis & Tononi (2014)** — *From the phenomenology to the mechanisms of consciousness: Integrated Information Theory 3.0.* PLoS Computational Biology. Integrated information theory, practical formalism. [doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003588](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003588)
- **Albantakis et al. (2023)** — *Integrated Information Theory (IIT) 4.0.* PLoS Computational Biology. Latest IIT formulation; intrinsic information. [doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465)
