# Renormalization on Networks

Coarse-graining networks and higher-order structures across scales while
preserving what matters — dynamics, information, predictability. The
across-agent analogue of the RG that organizes statistical mechanics.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Villegas, Gili, Mulder, Caldarelli & Bianconi (2023)** — *Laplacian renormalization group for heterogeneous networks.* Nature Physics. Diffusion-based spectral coarse-graining. The key recent paper.
- **García-Pérez, Boguñá & Serrano (2018)** — *Multiscale unfolding of real networks by geometric renormalization.* Nature Physics. Geometric (hidden-metric) approach to network RG.
- **Serrano, Krioukov & Boguñá (2008)** — *Self-similarity of complex networks and hidden metric spaces.* Physical Review Letters 100, 078701 Hidden hyperbolic geometry underpinning geometric RG.
- **Koch-Janusz & Ringel (2018)** — *Mutual information, neural networks and the renormalization group.* Nature Physics. Information-theoretic / ML-driven RG.
- **Nurisso, Morandini, Lucas, Vaccarino, Gili & Petri (2025)** — *Higher-order Laplacian renormalization.* Nature Physics. Cross-order Laplacians; order-specific scale invariance.
- **Reitz & Bianconi (2020)** — *The higher-order spectrum of simplicial complexes: a renormalization group approach.* J. Phys. A. Higher-order spectral RG.
- **Guadagnuolo, Nurisso, Galluzzi, Allard & Petri (2026)** — *Harmonic morphisms and dynamical invariants in network renormalization.* arXiv:2604.08386 (under review, PRX). Discrete harmonic morphisms as the minimal criterion for preserving random-walk dynamics under coarse-graining.
- ⭐ **Murphy, Thibeault, Allard & Desrosiers (2024)** — *Duality between predictability and reconstructability in complex systems.* Nature Communications. Coarse-graining trades reconstructability for predictability — a causal-emergence signature.
- **Gabrielli, Garlaschelli, Patil & Serrano (2025)** — *Network renormalization.* Nature Reviews Physics 7, 203–219 Comprehensive review of geometric, Laplacian and spectral RG on networks.
- **Bradde & Bialek (2017)** — *PCA meets RG.* Journal of Statistical Physics. PCA and renormalization as the same idea.
