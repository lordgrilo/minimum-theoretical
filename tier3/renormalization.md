# Renormalization on Networks

Coarse-graining networks and higher-order structures across scales while preserving
what matters — dynamics, information, predictability. The network analogue of the
renormalization group that organizes statistical mechanics.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Villegas, Gili, Caldarelli & Gabrielli (2023)** — *Laplacian renormalization group for heterogeneous networks.* Nature Physics. Diffusion-based spectral coarse-graining. The key recent paper. [doi:10.1038/s41567-022-01866-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-022-01866-8)
- **García-Pérez, Boguñá & Serrano (2018)** — *Multiscale unfolding of real networks by geometric renormalization.* Nature Physics. Geometric (hidden-metric) approach to network RG. [doi:10.1038/s41567-018-0072-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-018-0072-5)
- **Serrano, Krioukov & Boguñá (2008)** — *Self-similarity of complex networks and hidden metric spaces.* Physical Review Letters 100, 078701 Hidden hyperbolic geometry underpinning geometric RG. [doi:10.1103/physrevlett.100.078701](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.100.078701)
