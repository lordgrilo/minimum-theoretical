# Clinical & Developmental Higher-Order Information

Higher-order / synergistic information as a within-agent biomarker:
synergy structure that pairwise analysis misses, applied to consciousness,
psychiatry, epilepsy and development. Ties the brain-topology work to our new
clinical and developmental projects.

## Reading

- **Varley, Pope, Faskowitz & Sporns (2023)** — *Multivariate information theory uncovers synergistic subsystems of the human cerebral cortex.* Communications Biology 6:451 O-information identifies widespread synergistic subsystems in resting-state fMRI.
- **Luppi, Mediano, Rosas et al. (2024)** — *A synergistic workspace for human consciousness revealed by Integrated Information Decomposition.* eLife 12:RP88173 Synergistic 'workspace' (gateways + broadcasters) supporting consciousness; reconciles GNWT and IIT.
- **Mirjebreili, Martinez de Aguirre Ibarreta, Marinazzo & Chauvière (2025)** — *High-order information analysis of epileptogenesis in the pilocarpine rat model of temporal lobe epilepsy.* eNeuro 12(5) O-information / synergy multiplets track epileptogenesis. Anchor for our epilepsy work. NB: rat TLE model, not human iEEG.
- **Li, Calhoun, Ballem, Iraji, Yu & Malo (2023)** — *Aberrant high-order dependencies in schizophrenia resting-state fMRI networks.* arXiv:2310.17445 (NeurIPS 2023 workshop) O-information / S-information on schizophrenia rs-fMRI. Anchor for our schizophrenia work. NB: workshop/preprint.
- **Marek et al. (2022)** — *Reproducible brain-wide association studies require thousands of individuals.* Nature. Sample-size and reproducibility reality check for brain–behaviour studies.
- **Marquand, Rezek, Buitelaar & Beckmann (2016)** — *Understanding heterogeneity in clinical cohorts using normative models.* Biological Psychiatry. Normative modelling: individual deviation, not group means.
- **Power, Barnes, Snyder, Schlaggar & Petersen (2012)** — *Spurious but systematic correlations in functional connectivity MRI networks arise from subject motion.* NeuroImage. The motion confound every fMRI-network claim must address.
- **Di Martino et al. (2014)** — *Unraveling the miswired connectome: a developmental perspective.* Neuron. Developmental connectomics framing.
