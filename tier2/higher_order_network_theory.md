# Higher-Order Network Theory

The mathematical core: describing, modelling, analysing and inferring higher-order
interactions among agents.

## Reading

- **Lim (2020)** — *Hodge Laplacians on graphs.* SIAM Review. Hodge decomposition on simplicial complexes — generalizing the graph Laplacian. [doi:10.1137/18m1223101](https://doi.org/10.1137/18m1223101)
- **Schaub, Benson, Horn, Lippner & Jadbabaie (2020)** — *Random walks on simplicial complexes and the normalized Hodge 1-Laplacian.* SIAM Review. How random walks generalize to higher-order structures. [doi:10.1137/18m1201019](https://doi.org/10.1137/18m1201019)
- ⭐ **Iacopini, Petri, Barrat & Latora (2019)** — *Simplicial models of social contagion.* Nature Communications. Group interactions create bistability and discontinuous transitions. Founding paper. [doi:10.1038/s41467-019-10431-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-10431-6)
- **Petri & Barrat (2018)** — *Simplicial activity driven model.* Physical Review Letters. Temporal higher-order network model. [doi:10.1103/physrevlett.121.228301](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.121.228301)
- **Perra, Gonçalves, Pastor-Satorras & Vespignani (2012)** — *Activity driven modeling of time varying networks.* Scientific Reports 2, 469. The minimal generative model for temporal networks, and the pairwise baseline behind Petri & Barrat 2018. [doi:10.1038/srep00469](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep00469)
- **Valdano, Ferreri, Poletto & Colizza (2015)** — *Analytical computation of the epidemic threshold on temporal networks.* Physical Review X 5, 021005. The epidemic threshold when the contact network itself changes in time. [doi:10.1103/physrevx.5.021005](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevx.5.021005)
- **De Domenico, Solé-Ribalta, Cozzo, Kivelä, Moreno, Porter, Gómez & Arenas (2013)** — *Mathematical formulation of multilayer networks.* Physical Review X 3, 041022. The tensor formalism behind Kivelä et al. (2014). Read it when a project needs multilayer operators (Laplacians, centralities), not just the vocabulary. [doi:10.1103/physrevx.3.041022](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevx.3.041022)
- **De Domenico, Nicosia, Arenas & Latora (2015)** — *Structural reducibility of multilayer networks.* Nature Communications 6, 6864. When can layers be merged without losing information? An information-theoretic test. [doi:10.1038/ncomms7864](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms7864)
- **Lucas, Iacopini, Robiglio, Barrat & Petri (2023)** — *Simplicially driven simple contagion.* Physical Review Research. Even simple contagion changes on simplicial complexes. [doi:10.1103/physrevresearch.5.013201](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevresearch.5.013201)
- **Mancastroppa, Iacopini, Petri & Barrat (2023)** — *Hyper-cores promote localization and efficient seeding in higher-order processes.* Nature Communications. Core decomposition for higher-order spreading. [doi:10.1038/s41467-023-41887-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-41887-2)
- **Skardal & Arenas (2019)** — *Abrupt desynchronization and extensive multistability in globally coupled oscillator simplexes.* PRL. Higher-order coupling creates explosive synchronization. [doi:10.1103/physrevlett.122.248301](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.122.248301)
- **Millán, Torres & Bianconi (2020)** — *Explosive higher-order Kuramoto dynamics on simplicial complexes.* PRL. Synchronization with discontinuous transitions. [doi:10.1103/physrevlett.124.218301](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.124.218301)
- **Nurisso, Arnaudon, Lucas, Peach, Expert, Vaccarino & Petri (2024)** — *A unified framework for simplicial Kuramoto models.* Chaos. A unified framework for simplicial Kuramoto models (lab paper). [doi:10.1063/5.0169388](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0169388)
- **Kuramoto (1984)** — *Chemical Oscillations, Waves, and Turbulence.* Springer. The synchronization reference. [doi:10.1007/978-3-642-69689-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-69689-3)
- ⭐ **Arenas, Díaz-Guilera, Kurths, Moreno & Zhou (2008)** — *Synchronization in complex networks.* Physics Reports. Comprehensive synchronization review. [doi:10.1016/j.physrep.2008.09.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2008.09.002)
- ⭐ **Young, Petri & Peixoto (2021)** — *Hypergraph reconstruction from network data.* Communications Physics. Bayesian framework for inferring higher-order structure. [doi:10.1038/s42005-021-00637-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-021-00637-w)
- **Preti, Fazzone, Petri & De Francisci Morales (2024)** — *Higher-order null models as a lens for social systems.* Physical Review X. Higher-order null models; US Congress cosponsorship as primary case. [doi:10.1103/PhysRevX.14.031032](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.14.031032)
- **Rosas, Mediano, Luppi, Varley, Lizier, Stramaglia, Jensen & Marinazzo (2022)** — *Disentangling high-order mechanisms and high-order behaviours in complex systems.* Nature Physics 18, 476–477. Higher-order statistics do not imply higher-order mechanisms, nor the reverse: the distinction every higher-order claim has to respect. [doi:10.1038/s41567-022-01548-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-022-01548-5)
- **Malizia, Corso, Gambuzza, Russo, Latora & Frasca (2024)** — *Reconstructing higher-order interactions in coupled dynamical systems.* Nature Communications 15, 5184. Infers pairwise and higher-order interactions from the time series of coupled dynamical systems. [doi:10.1038/s41467-024-49278-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49278-x)
