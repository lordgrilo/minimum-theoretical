# Higher-Order Network Theory

The mathematical core of the lab's programme: describing, modelling, analysing and
inferring higher-order interactions among agents. The machinery of across-agent structure.

## Reading

- **Lim (2020)** — *Hodge Laplacians on graphs.* SIAM Review. Hodge decomposition on simplicial complexes — generalizing the graph Laplacian.
- **Schaub, Benson, Horn, Lippner & Jadbabaie (2020)** — *Random walks on simplicial complexes and the normalized Hodge 1-Laplacian.* SIAM Review. How random walks generalize to higher-order structures.
- ⭐ **Iacopini, Petri, Barrat & Latora (2019)** — *Simplicial models of social contagion.* Nature Communications. Group interactions create bistability and discontinuous transitions. Founding paper.
- **Petri & Barrat (2018)** — *Simplicial activity driven model.* Physical Review Letters. Temporal higher-order network model.
- **Perra, Gonçalves, Pastor-Satorras & Vespignani (2012)** — *Activity driven modeling of time varying networks.* Scientific Reports 2, 469. The minimal generative model for temporal networks, and the pairwise baseline behind Petri & Barrat 2018.
- **Valdano, Ferreri, Poletto & Colizza (2015)** — *Analytical computation of the epidemic threshold on temporal networks.* Physical Review X 5, 021005. The epidemic threshold when the contact network itself changes in time.
- **De Domenico, Solé-Ribalta, Cozzo, Kivelä, Moreno, Porter, Gómez & Arenas (2013)** — *Mathematical formulation of multilayer networks.* Physical Review X 3, 041022. The tensor formalism for multilayer networks.
- **De Domenico, Nicosia, Arenas & Latora (2015)** — *Structural reducibility of multilayer networks.* Nature Communications 6, 6864. When can layers be merged without losing information? An information-theoretic test.
- **Lucas, Iacopini, Robiglio, Barrat & Petri (2023)** — *Simplicially driven simple contagion.* Physical Review Research. Even simple contagion changes on simplicial complexes.
- **Mancastroppa, Iacopini, Petri & Barrat (2023)** — *Hyper-cores promote localization and efficient seeding in higher-order processes.* Nature Communications. Core decomposition for higher-order spreading.
- **Skardal & Arenas (2019)** — *Abrupt desynchronization and extensive multistability in globally coupled oscillator simplexes.* PRL. Higher-order coupling creates explosive synchronization.
- **Millán, Torres & Bianconi (2020)** — *Explosive higher-order Kuramoto dynamics on simplicial complexes.* PRL. Synchronization with discontinuous transitions.
- **Nurisso, Vaccarino & Petri (2024)** — *A unified framework for simplicial Kuramoto models.* Chaos. NPLab unification of simplicial Kuramoto.
- **Kuramoto (1984)** — *Chemical Oscillations, Waves, and Turbulence.* Springer. The synchronization reference.
- ⭐ **Arenas, Díaz-Guilera, Kurths, Moreno & Zhou (2008)** — *Synchronization in complex networks.* Physics Reports. Comprehensive synchronization review.
- ⭐ **Young, Petri & Peixoto (2021)** — *Hypergraph reconstruction from network data.* Communications Physics. Bayesian framework for inferring higher-order structure.
- **Preti, De Francisci Morales, Iacopini & Petri (2024)** — *Higher-order null models as a lens for social systems.* Physical Review X. Higher-order null models; US Congress cosponsorship as primary case.
