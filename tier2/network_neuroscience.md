# Network Neuroscience & Brain Topology

How brain structure and topology shape function, and what topological tools reveal
that standard network metrics miss. (Neural-coding textbooks are now in Tier 1:
Representations & Neural Coding.)

## Reading

- **Bassett & Sporns (2017)** — *Network neuroscience.* Nature Neuroscience. Established the field. [doi:10.1038/nn.4502](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4502)
- **Luppi, Mediano, Rosas, Holland, Fryer, O'Brien, Rowe, Menon, Bor & Stamatakis (2022)** — *A synergistic core for human brain evolution and cognition.* Nature Neuroscience 25, 771–782. Where synergy and redundancy sit in the human brain, measured with integrated information decomposition. [doi:10.1038/s41593-022-01070-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-022-01070-0)
- **Gava, Lefèvre et al. (2024)** — *Organizing the coactivity structure of the hippocampus from robust to flexible memory.* Science 385, 1120–1127. Rodent electrophysiology on how hippocampal coactivity structure trades robustness against flexibility as memories form. Case material: ask which part of the structure survives a firing-rate or pairwise-correlation null. [doi:10.1126/science.adk9611](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adk9611)
- **Sun, Winnubst et al. (2025)** — *Learning produces an orthogonalized state machine in the hippocampus.* Nature 640, 165–175. How learning reorganizes hippocampal activity into orthogonalized, state-machine-like representations. Case material: ask whether a latent-state model is needed, or whether slow drift or a task-variable code explains the same orthogonalization. [doi:10.1038/s41586-024-08548-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08548-w)
- **Bullmore & Sporns (2009)** — *Complex brain networks: graph theoretical analysis of structural and functional systems.* Nature Reviews Neuroscience. Accessible earlier review; small-world, hubs, modularity. [doi:10.1038/nrn2575](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2575)
- **Sporns (2010)** — *Networks of the Brain.* MIT Press. Book-length treatment of brain network science. [doi:10.7551/mitpress/8476.001.0001](https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/8476.001.0001)
- **Hubel & Wiesel (1962)** — *Receptive fields, binocular interaction and functional architecture in the cat's visual cortex.* Journal of Physiology. Foundational experimental paper on neural coding. [doi:10.1113/jphysiol.1962.sp006837](https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.1962.sp006837)
- **Georgopoulos, Schwartz & Kettner (1986)** — *Neuronal population coding of movement direction.* Science. Population vector coding. [doi:10.1126/science.3749885](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.3749885)
- **Pouget, Dayan & Zemel (2000)** — *Information processing with population codes.* Nature Reviews Neuroscience. How populations encode and decode information. [doi:10.1038/35039062](https://doi.org/10.1038/35039062)
- **Averbeck, Latham & Pouget (2006)** — *Neural correlations, population coding and computation.* Nature Reviews Neuroscience. Role of correlations in population coding. [doi:10.1038/nrn1888](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn1888)
- **Santoro, Battiston, Petri & Amico (2023)** — *Higher-order organization of multivariate time series.* Nature Physics. Higher-order connectomics methodology. [doi:10.1038/s41567-022-01852-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-022-01852-0)
- **Santoro, Battiston, Lucas, Petri & Amico (2024)** — *Higher-order connectomics of human brain function.* Nature Communications. Triplet interactions enhance task decoding and brain fingerprinting beyond pairwise. [doi:10.1038/s41467-024-54472-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-54472-y)
- **Patania, Selvaggi, Veronese, Dipasquale, Expert & Petri (2019)** — *Topological gene expression networks recapitulate brain anatomy and function.* Network Neuroscience. Alice Patania — now Indiana University. [doi:10.1162/netn_a_00094](https://doi.org/10.1162/netn_a_00094)
- **Gatica et al. (2024)** — *Transcranial ultrasound stimulation effect in the redundant and synergistic networks consistent across macaques.* Network Neuroscience. Gatica–Petri collaboration. [doi:10.1162/netn_a_00388](https://doi.org/10.1162/netn_a_00388)
- **Lord, Expert, Fernandes, Petri et al. (2016)** — *Insights into brain architectures from the homological scaffolds of functional connectivity networks.* Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience. Mesoscopic brain organization via scaffolds. [doi:10.3389/fnsys.2016.00085](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnsys.2016.00085)
- **Billings, Saggar, Hlinka, Keilholz & Petri (2021)** — *Simplicial and topological descriptions of human brain dynamics.* Network Neuroscience. Topological description of brain dynamics. [doi:10.1162/netn_a_00190](https://doi.org/10.1162/netn_a_00190)
- **Clopath, Büsing, Vasilaki & Gerstner (2010)** — *Connectivity reflects coding: a model of voltage-based STDP with homeostasis.* Nature Neuroscience. Voltage-based STDP rule. [doi:10.1038/nn.2479](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2479)
- **Sadeh & Clopath (2025)** — *The emergence of NeuroAI: bridging neuroscience and artificial intelligence.* Nature Reviews Neuroscience. Directly relevant to the NeuroAI stream. [doi:10.1038/s41583-025-00954-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41583-025-00954-x)
- **Schneidman, Berry, Segev & Bialek (2006)** — *Weak pairwise correlations imply strongly correlated network states in a neural population.* Nature 440, 1007–1012. Pairwise maximum-entropy models capture most of the correlated states of small retinal populations: the classic case that pairs suffice. [doi:10.1038/nature04701](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04701)
- **Ganmor, Segev & Schneidman (2011)** — *Sparse low-order interaction network underlies a highly correlated and learnable neural population code.* PNAS 108, 9679–9684. In larger populations pairwise models fall short; a sparse set of low-order interactions describes the code. [doi:10.1073/pnas.1019641108](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1019641108)
- **Finn, Shen, Scheinost, Rosenberg, Huang, Chun, Papademetris & Constable (2015)** — *Functional connectome fingerprinting: identifying individuals using patterns of brain connectivity.* Nature Neuroscience 18, 1664–1671. Pairwise functional connectivity identifies individuals across sessions. [doi:10.1038/nn.4135](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.4135)
- **Noble, Scheinost & Constable (2019)** — *A decade of test-retest reliability of functional connectivity: a systematic review and meta-analysis.* NeuroImage 203, 116157. How reliable functional-connectivity edges are, and what makes them so. [doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.116157](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.116157)
