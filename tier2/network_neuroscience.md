# Network Neuroscience & Brain Topology

Information processing inside the biological agent. How brain structure and
topology shape function — and how the lab's topological tools reveal structure
standard metrics miss. (Neural-coding textbooks are now in Tier 1:
Representations & Neural Coding.)

## Reading

- **Bassett & Sporns (2017)** — *Network neuroscience.* Nature Neuroscience. Established the field.
- **Bullmore & Sporns (2009)** — *Complex brain networks: graph theoretical analysis of structural and functional systems.* Nature Reviews Neuroscience. Accessible earlier review; small-world, hubs, modularity.
- **Sporns (2010)** — *Networks of the Brain.* MIT Press. Book-length treatment of brain network science.
- **Hubel & Wiesel (1962)** — *Receptive fields, binocular interaction and functional architecture in the cat's visual cortex.* Journal of Physiology. Foundational experimental paper on neural coding.
- **Georgopoulos, Schwartz & Kettner (1986)** — *Neuronal population coding of movement direction.* Science. Population vector coding.
- **Pouget, Dayan & Zemel (2000)** — *Information processing with population codes.* Nature Reviews Neuroscience. How populations encode and decode information.
- **Averbeck, Latham & Pouget (2006)** — *Neural correlations, population coding and computation.* Nature Reviews Neuroscience. Role of correlations in population coding.
- **Santoro, Battiston, Petri & Amico (2023)** — *Higher-order organization of multivariate time series.* Nature Physics. Higher-order connectomics methodology.
- **Santoro, Battiston, Lucas, Petri & Amico (2024)** — *Higher-order connectomics of human brain function.* Nature Communications. Triplet interactions enhance task decoding and brain fingerprinting beyond pairwise.
- **Patania, Selvaggi, Veronese, Dipasquale, Expert & Petri (2019)** — *Topological gene expression networks recapitulate brain anatomy and function.* Network Neuroscience. Alice Patania — now Indiana University.
- **Gatica et al. (2024)** — *Transcranial ultrasound stimulation effect in the redundant and synergistic networks consistent across macaques.* Network Neuroscience. Gatica–Petri collaboration.
- **Lord, Expert, Fernandes, Petri et al. (2016)** — *Insights into brain architectures from the homological scaffolds of functional connectivity networks.* Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience. Mesoscopic brain organization via scaffolds.
- **Billings, Saggar, Keilholz, Petri & Mantzaris (2021)** — *Simplicial and topological descriptions of human brain dynamics.* Network Neuroscience. Topological description of brain dynamics.
- **Clopath, Büsing, Vasilaki & Gerstner (2010)** — *Connectivity reflects coding: a model of voltage-based STDP with homeostasis.* Nature Neuroscience. Voltage-based STDP rule.
- **Sadeh & Clopath (2025)** — *The emergence of NeuroAI: bridging neuroscience and artificial intelligence.* Nature Reviews Neuroscience. Directly relevant to the lab's NeuroAI stream.
