# VLM / Representation Geometry & the Binding Problem

How neural networks represent compositional structure, why the binding problem
recurs in deep models, and the geometry of concept representations in LLMs/VLMs.

## Reading

- **von der Malsburg (1981)** — *The correlation theory of brain function.* MPI internal report 81-2 (repr. 1994 in *Models of Neural Networks II*, Springer). The classical statement of the binding problem; temporal correlation as a binding mechanism. [doi:10.1007/978-1-4612-4320-5_2](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4612-4320-5_2)
- **Greff, van Steenkiste & Schmidhuber (2020)** — *On the binding problem in artificial neural networks.* arXiv:2012.05208. Unifying framing of dynamic binding as a core limitation of neural representations. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2012.05208](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2012.05208) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.05208)
- **Plate (1995)** — *Holographic reduced representations.* IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks. Circular-convolution binding; the VSA alternative to TPR with unbinding. (Pairs with Smolensky.) [doi:10.1109/72.377968](https://doi.org/10.1109/72.377968)
- **McCoy, Linzen, Dunbar & Smolensky (2019)** — *RNNs implicitly implement tensor-product representations.* ICLR. Trained RNNs learn TPR-like compositional encodings — bridge to classical theory. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1812.08718](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1812.08718) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.08718)
- **Elhage, Hume, Olsson et al. (2022)** — *Toy models of superposition.* Anthropic / Transformer Circuits. Polysemanticity via feature compression; phase transitions in representation geometry. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2209.10652](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2209.10652) [link](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/toy_model/index.html)
- **Locatello, Weissenborn, Unterthiner et al. (2020)** — *Object-centric learning with slot attention.* NeurIPS. Slot-based object discovery; unsupervised compositional decomposition. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2006.15055](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.15055) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15055)
- **Templeton, Conerly et al. (2024)** — *Scaling monosemanticity: extracting interpretable features from Claude 3 Sonnet.* Anthropic / Transformer Circuits. Sparse autoencoders decompose representations into monosemantic features. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2605.29358](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.29358) [link](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2024/scaling-monosemanticity/index.html)
- **Huh, Cheung, Wang & Isola (2024)** — *Position: The Platonic representation hypothesis.* ICML 2024. arXiv:2405.07987 Disparate networks converge to a shared statistical representation of reality. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2405.07987](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2405.07987) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07987)
- **Pach, Bader, Bouniot, Belongie & Akata (2026)** — *The latent color subspace: emergent order in high-dimensional chaos.* arXiv:2603.12261. Emergent low-dimensional color geometry inside generative latent spaces — representational order arising without supervision. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.12261](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.12261) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12261)
- **Solé, Seoane, Pla-Mauri, Bennett, Hochberg & Levin (2026)** — *Cognition spaces: natural, artificial, and hybrid.* arXiv:2601.12837. A morphospace of cognition spanning biological, artificial and hybrid agents — the landscape of representations at its broadest. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2601.12837](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.12837) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12837)
- **Bowers, Malhotra, Dujmović, Llera Montero, Tsvetkov, Biscione, Puebla, Adolfi, Hummel, Heaton, Evans, Mitchell & Blything (2023)** — *Deep problems with neural network models of human vision.* Behavioral and Brain Sciences 46, e385. Argues that benchmark and brain-prediction successes overstate how well deep networks model human vision. [doi:10.1017/s0140525x22002813](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0140525x22002813)
- **Park, Choe & Veitch (2024)** — *The linear representation hypothesis and the geometry of large language models.* ICML 2024. arXiv:2311.03658 Formalises what it means for a concept to be a direction, and the geometry this induces in language models. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2311.03658](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2311.03658) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03658)
- **Campbell, Rane, Giallanza, De Sabbata, Ghods, Joshi, Ku, Frankland, Griffiths, Cohen & Webb (2024)** — *Understanding the limits of vision language models through the lens of the binding problem.* NeurIPS 2024. arXiv:2411.00238 Multi-object failures of vision–language models behave like the binding problem in human vision. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2411.00238](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2411.00238) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00238)
