# Network Processes, Inference & Robustness

Dynamical processes on networks and the inferential turn: epidemics and percolation,
robustness and cascading failure, and the shift from describing network structure to
inferring it. Working depth for network projects — not part of the Tier 1 minimum.

## Reading

- **Albert & Barabási (2002)** — *Statistical mechanics of complex networks.* Reviews of Modern Physics. Framed network science as a branch of statistical physics. [doi:10.1103/revmodphys.74.47](https://doi.org/10.1103/revmodphys.74.47)
- **Fortunato (2010)** — *Community detection in graphs.* Physics Reports. Comprehensive review of community detection. [doi:10.1016/j.physrep.2009.11.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2009.11.002)
- ⭐ **Barrat, Barthélemy & Vespignani (2008)** — *Dynamical Processes on Complex Networks.* Cambridge. Essential companion to Newman. First pass: Ch. 4–7 and 9 (phase transitions, resilience and robustness, synchronization, epidemic spreading). [doi:10.1017/cbo9780511791383](https://doi.org/10.1017/cbo9780511791383)
- ⭐ **Pastor-Satorras & Vespignani (2001)** — *Epidemic spreading in scale-free networks.* PRL. Absence of an epidemic threshold in scale-free networks. [doi:10.1103/physrevlett.86.3200](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.86.3200)
- **Pastor-Satorras, Castellano, Van Mieghem & Vespignani (2015)** — *Epidemic processes in complex networks.* Reviews of Modern Physics. Definitive review of two decades of epidemic spreading. [doi:10.1103/revmodphys.87.925](https://doi.org/10.1103/revmodphys.87.925)
- ⭐ **Newman (2002)** — *Spread of epidemic disease on networks.* Physical Review E. Maps SIR epidemics onto bond percolation. [doi:10.1103/physreve.66.016128](https://doi.org/10.1103/physreve.66.016128)
- ⭐ **Molloy & Reed (1995)** — *A critical point for random graphs with a given degree sequence.* Random Structures & Algorithms. The Molloy–Reed criterion for the giant component. [doi:10.1002/rsa.3240060204](https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.3240060204)
- ⭐ **Callaway, Newman, Strogatz & Watts (2000)** — *Network robustness and fragility: percolation on random graphs.* PRL. Robustness as percolation. [doi:10.1103/physrevlett.85.5468](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.85.5468)
- ⭐ **Albert, Jeong & Barabási (2000)** — *Error and attack tolerance of complex networks.* Nature. Landmark robustness paper. [doi:10.1038/35019019](https://doi.org/10.1038/35019019)
- ⭐ **Buldyrev, Parshani, Paul, Stanley & Havlin (2010)** — *Catastrophic cascade of failures in interdependent networks.* Nature. Landmark interdependent-networks paper. [doi:10.1038/nature08932](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08932)
- ⭐ **Peel, Peixoto & De Domenico (2022)** — *Statistical inference links data and theory in network science.* Nature Communications 13, 6794. The shift from descriptive to inferential network science. [doi:10.1038/s41467-022-34267-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-34267-9)
- **Prasse & Van Mieghem (2022)** — *Predicting network dynamics without requiring the knowledge of the interaction graph.* PNAS 119, e2205517119. Network dynamics predicted accurately without reconstructing the interaction graph. [doi:10.1073/pnas.2205517119](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2205517119)
- **Thibeault, Allard & Desrosiers (2024)** — *The low-rank hypothesis of complex systems.* Nature Physics 20, 294–302. Real networks have low effective rank, which justifies low-dimensional descriptions of their dynamics. [doi:10.1038/s41567-023-02303-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-023-02303-0)
