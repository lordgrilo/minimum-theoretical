# Animal Communication & CETI

Information processing across communicating agents in the wild: the structure of
animal signals, compositionality across taxa, and Project CETI's analysis of
sperm-whale codas. Communication as a rate-distortion problem between agents.

## Reading

- **Rendell & Whitehead (2001)** — *Culture in whales and dolphins.* Behavioral and Brain Sciences. Cultural transmission of vocal repertoires.
- **Gero, Whitehead & Rendell (2016)** — *Individual, unit and vocal clan level identity cues in sperm whale codas.* Royal Society Open Science. Hierarchical social identity in coda patterns.
- **Andreas, Beguš, Bronstein, Diamant, Delaney et al. (2022)** — *Toward understanding the communication in sperm whales.* iScience. CETI collaboration overview.
- **Sharma, Gero, Payne, Gruber, Rus, Torralba & Andreas (2024)** — *Contextual and combinatorial structure in sperm whale vocalisations.* Nature Communications 15, 3617. Codas have productive combinatorial structure (the 'phonetic alphabet').
- **Beguš, Dąbkowski, Sprouse, Gruber & Gero (2026)** — *The phonology of sperm whale coda vowels.* Proceedings of the Royal Society B 293:20252994 Vowel/diphthong-like patterns with phonological parallels to human language.
- **Youngblood (2025)** — *Language-like efficiency in whale communication.* Science Advances 11:eads6014 Menzerath's and Zipf's laws across cetacean species.
- **Hauser, Chomsky & Fitch (2002)** — *The faculty of language: what is it, who has it, and how did it evolve?* Science. The FLB/FLN framing; comparative context for animal communication.
- **Kirby, Cornish & Smith (2008)** — *Cumulative cultural evolution in the laboratory.* PNAS. Experimental iterated learning; structure from transmission.
- **Townsend et al. (2018)** — *Compositionality in animals and humans.* PLoS Biology. Comprehensive compositionality review across taxa.
- **Kershenbaum et al. (2016)** — *Acoustic sequences in non-human animals: a tutorial review and prospectus.* Biological Reviews. The 40+-author framework for sequence analysis.
- **Sainburg, Thielk & Gentner (2020)** — *Finding, visualizing, and quantifying latent structure across diverse animal vocal repertoires.* PLoS Computational Biology. Latent-space methods for vocal repertoires.
