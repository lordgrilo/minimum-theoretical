# Machine Learning & Geometric / Topological Deep Learning

Used on both sides of the throughline. The ML bridge for a maths/physics
audience, geometric and topological deep learning (DL on graphs, simplicial and
cell complexes), and the scaling/generalization phenomena the semanticity
framework aims to explain.

## Reading

- **Murphy (2022)** — *Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction.* MIT Press. Best single choice for a mathematical audience. Free online.
- **Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville (2016)** — *Deep Learning.* MIT Press. Part I (Ch. 2–5) as the applied-maths bridge. Free online.
- **Kipf & Welling (2017)** — *Semi-supervised classification with graph convolutional networks.* ICLR. The GCN paper; entry point to graph neural networks.
- **Hamilton (2020)** — *Graph Representation Learning.* Morgan & Claypool. Free online; the GRL reference.
- **Bronstein, Bruna, Cohen & Veličković (2021)** — *Geometric Deep Learning: Grids, Groups, Graphs, Geodesics, and Gauges.* arXiv:2104.13478. Unifying framework for DL on non-Euclidean domains.
- **Bodnar, Frasca, Otter, Wang, Liò, Montúfar & Veličković (2021)** — *Weisfeiler and Leman go topological: message passing simplicial networks.* ICML. GNNs on simplicial complexes.
- **Papillon, Sanborn, Hajij & Miolane (2023)** — *Architectures of topological deep learning: a survey of message-passing topological neural networks.* arXiv:2304.10031 NEW: the survey for topological deep learning beyond graphs.
- **Kaplan et al. (2020)** — *Scaling laws for neural language models.* arXiv:2001.08361. Empirical scaling laws (size, data, compute).
- **Nakkiran et al. (2021)** — *Deep double descent.* JMLR / ICLR. Non-monotonic generalization curves — a semanticity prediction.
- **Belkin, Hsu, Ma & Mandal (2019)** — *Reconciling modern machine-learning practice and the classical bias–variance trade-off.* PNAS. Brought double descent to the mainstream.
- **Shwartz-Ziv & Tishby (2017)** — *Opening the black box of deep neural networks via information.* arXiv:1703.00810. Information Bottleneck applied to DL training dynamics.
