# Machine Learning & Geometric / Topological Deep Learning

Used throughout the lab. The ML bridge for a maths/physics audience, geometric and
topological deep learning (DL on graphs, simplicial and cell complexes), and the
scaling/generalization phenomena the semanticity framework aims to explain.

## Reading

- **Murphy (2022)** — *Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction.* MIT Press. Best single choice for a mathematical audience. Free online. [link](https://probml.github.io/pml-book/book1.html)
- **Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville (2016)** — *Deep Learning.* MIT Press. Part I (Ch. 2–5) as the applied-maths bridge. Free online. [link](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/)
- **Kipf & Welling (2017)** — *Semi-supervised classification with graph convolutional networks.* ICLR. The GCN paper; entry point to graph neural networks. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1609.02907](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1609.02907) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02907)
- **Hamilton (2020)** — *Graph Representation Learning.* Morgan & Claypool. Free online; the GRL reference. [doi:10.1007/978-3-031-01588-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-01588-5)
- **Bronstein, Bruna, Cohen & Veličković (2021)** — *Geometric Deep Learning: Grids, Groups, Graphs, Geodesics, and Gauges.* arXiv:2104.13478. Unifying framework for DL on non-Euclidean domains. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2104.13478](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2104.13478) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.13478)
- **Bodnar, Frasca, Wang, Otter, Montúfar, Liò & Bronstein (2021)** — *Weisfeiler and Lehman go topological: message passing simplicial networks.* ICML. GNNs on simplicial complexes. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2103.03212](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2103.03212) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03212)
- **Papillon, Sanborn, Hajij & Miolane (2023)** — *Architectures of topological deep learning: a survey of message-passing topological neural networks.* arXiv:2304.10031 NEW: the survey for topological deep learning beyond graphs. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2304.10031](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2304.10031) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.10031)
- **Kaplan et al. (2020)** — *Scaling laws for neural language models.* arXiv:2001.08361. Empirical scaling laws (size, data, compute). [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2001.08361](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2001.08361) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361)
- **Nakkiran et al. (2021)** — *Deep double descent: where bigger models and more data hurt.* J. Stat. Mech. 2021, 124003 (ICLR 2020). Non-monotonic generalization curves — a semanticity prediction. [doi:10.1088/1742-5468/ac3a74](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/ac3a74)
- **Belkin, Hsu, Ma & Mandal (2019)** — *Reconciling modern machine-learning practice and the classical bias–variance trade-off.* PNAS. Brought double descent to the mainstream. [doi:10.1073/pnas.1903070116](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1903070116)
- **Shwartz-Ziv & Tishby (2017)** — *Opening the black box of deep neural networks via information.* arXiv:1703.00810. Information Bottleneck applied to DL training dynamics. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1703.00810](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1703.00810) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.00810)
