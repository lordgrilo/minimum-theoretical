# Scientific Inference & Null Models

The lab's most common error mode is mistaking descriptive structure for a real
effect. This is the minimum for telling them apart — null models, permutation and
bootstrap tests, multiple comparisons, model checking and identifiability.
Required regardless of stream.

## Reading

- **Good (2005)** — *Permutation, Parametric, and Bootstrap Tests of Hypotheses* (3rd ed.). Springer. Resampling inference from first principles. [doi:10.1007/b138696](https://doi.org/10.1007/b138696)
- **Ernst (2004)** — *Permutation methods: a basis for exact inference.* Statistical Science 19, 676–685. Ten pages on why a permutation test is exact, and how to build one. [doi:10.1214/088342304000000396](https://doi.org/10.1214/088342304000000396)
- ⭐ **Fosdick, Larremore, Nishimura & Ugander (2018)** — *Configuring random graph models with fixed degree sequences.* SIAM Review. How to sample network null models correctly — the reference for graph nulls. [doi:10.1137/16m1087175](https://doi.org/10.1137/16m1087175)
- **Efron & Tibshirani (1993)** — *An Introduction to the Bootstrap.* Chapman & Hall. The bootstrap reference. [doi:10.1007/978-1-4899-4541-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4899-4541-9)
- **Benjamini & Hochberg (1995)** — *Controlling the false discovery rate.* Journal of the Royal Statistical Society B. Multiple comparisons done right (FDR). [doi:10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x)
- **Ioannidis (2005)** — *Why most published research findings are false.* PLoS Medicine. The epistemics of false positives. [doi:10.1371/journal.pmed.0020124](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.0020124)
