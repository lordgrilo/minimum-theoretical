# Computational Practice & Epistemic Hygiene

Reproducible, honest computation. The lab makes ambitious "universal law" claims;
this is the minimum not to fool ourselves — data leakage, ablations, effect sizes
over significance, preregistration, negative controls — plus the shared stack.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Kapoor & Narayanan (2023)** — *Leakage and the reproducibility crisis in machine-learning-based science.* Patterns. Data leakage — the dominant failure mode in ML-for-science.
- **Wilson, Bryan, Cranston, Kitzes, Nederbragt & Teal (2017)** — *Good enough practices in scientific computing.* PLoS Computational Biology. A reproducible-workflow baseline.
- ⭐ **Gelman & Loken (2013)** — *The garden of forking paths.* (working paper / American Scientist). Researcher degrees of freedom and the multiple-comparisons trap in exploratory work.
- **Nosek, Ebersole, DeHaven & Mellor (2018)** — *The preregistration revolution.* PNAS. When and why to preregister.
- **The lab's computational stack** — see the Tools page. Python + NumPy/SciPy, NetworkX/XGI/HyperNetX, GUDHI/Ripser/giotto-tda, dit/POT, nilearn/MNE, PyTorch. See tools/computational_tools.md.
