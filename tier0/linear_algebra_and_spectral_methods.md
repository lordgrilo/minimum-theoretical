# Linear Algebra & Spectral Methods

Almost every method in the lab is linear algebra underneath — PCA, spectral
clustering, graph Laplacians, RSA, manifold geometry, renormalization. Know
enough to use these without looking them up: eigenvalues/SVD, quadratic forms,
Laplacians, random walks and Markov chains, spectral embeddings.

## Reading

- **Strang (2016)** — *Introduction to Linear Algebra* (5th ed.). Wellesley–Cambridge. The standard first course: SVD, eigendecomposition, the four subspaces. [link](https://math.mit.edu/~gs/linearalgebra/)
- **Axler (2015)** — *Linear Algebra Done Right* (3rd ed.). Springer. Operator-first and conceptual; complements Strang. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-11080-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-11080-6)
- **Trefethen & Bau (1997)** — *Numerical Linear Algebra.* SIAM. SVD, conditioning, and the numerics that bite in practice. [doi:10.1137/1.9780898719574](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9780898719574)
- ⭐ **Spielman (2025)** — *Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory* (incomplete online draft dated 2 April 2025). https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/spielman/sagt/ Laplacians, effective resistance, spectral clustering — the bridge to networks and renormalization. [link](https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/spielman/sagt/)
