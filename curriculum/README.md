# Minimum Theoretical · the route

_Draft curriculum. Readings and exercises will change after the first pilot._

NPLab works across brains, animals, social systems and artificial agents with one toolkit: network science, higher-order networks and topology, with information theory as the common measure. This is the smallest body of theory that lets us read each other's work, ask useful questions, criticise results and move between projects. The name is a deliberate echo of Landau's Theoretical Minimum.

## How it works

Weeks 1–12 cover eight competencies. Each has foundation readings, one exercise and a checkpoint: you present your work at a Thursday lab meeting. Deepening and synthesis readings run alongside your project until the end of the first year. The provisional load is 8–10 hours a week; the first cohort will measure it.

Completion means you can:

1. explain the central construction or argument;
2. use it in a toy or project-relevant setting;
3. identify its assumptions and common failure modes;
4. distinguish descriptive structure from inference and mechanism;
5. connect it to at least one NPLab research programme;
6. state what evidence would weaken or falsify the associated claim.

## Tier 2 · Research Streams

_Months 4–8._ Go deep in one stream and stay literate in the others: one orientation reading per stream you do not follow. Streams are where your project lives.

- [Higher-order networks](streams/higher-order-networks.md). Build, analyse, and infer higher-order network models, and show in each case whether a pairwise or latent-variable alternative would suffice. Orientation reading for everyone else: Battiston et al. (2021).
- [Brain and clinical neuroscience](streams/brain-and-clinical-neuroscience.md). Connect neural measurement and preprocessing to network, topological, and multivariate-information claims, with reproducibility and confound checks. Orientation reading for everyone else: Bassett & Sporns (2017).
- [Cognition, semanticity and NeuroAI](streams/cognition-semanticity-and-neuroai.md). Relate learning objectives, cognitive control, and representational geometry in brains and machines to behavioural generalisation, and say what separates an interpretation from a mechanism. Orientation reading for everyone else: Sims (2016).
- [Social, collective and animal communication](streams/social-collective-and-animal-communication.md). Model coordination and communication across agents while respecting the different evidential standards of social experiments, field behaviour, and synthetic agents. Orientation reading for everyone else: Baronchelli, Felici, Loreto, Caglioti & Steels (2006).
- [Renormalization and criticality](streams/renormalization-and-criticality.md). Choose and test scale transformations, diagnose genuine critical behaviour, and quantify the trade-off between preserved dynamics, prediction, and reconstruction. Orientation reading for everyone else: Gabrielli, Garlaschelli, Patil & Serrano (2025).
