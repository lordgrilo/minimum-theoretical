# Higher-Order Structures

The mathematical core for everything beyond pairwise interactions. Tier 1: Battiston
2020 (the manifesto), Bianconi 2021 (the textbook). Tier 2: the rest.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Battiston, Cencetti, Iacopini, Latora, Lucas, Patania, Young & Petri (2020)** — *Networks beyond pairwise interactions: structure and dynamics.* Physics Reports. The manifesto. Defines the field. [doi:10.1016/j.physrep.2020.05.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2020.05.004)
- ⭐ **Kivelä, Arenas, Barthelemy, Gleeson, Moreno & Porter (2014)** — *Multilayer networks.* Journal of Complex Networks 2, 203–271. The formalism for networks with several kinds of edges or layers: when a multilayer description is needed, and what aggregating the layers throws away. [doi:10.1093/comnet/cnu016](https://doi.org/10.1093/comnet/cnu016)
- ⭐ **Holme & Saramäki (2012)** — *Temporal networks.* Physics Reports 519, 97–125. Edges that exist only at certain times: time-respecting paths, burstiness, and why aggregating over time changes what can spread. [doi:10.1016/j.physrep.2012.03.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physrep.2012.03.001)
- **Battiston et al. (2021)** — *The physics of higher-order interactions in complex systems.* Nature Physics. Perspective piece. [doi:10.1038/s41567-021-01371-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-021-01371-4)
- **Battiston, Bick, Lucas, Millán, Skardal & Zhang (2026)** — *Collective dynamics on higher-order networks.* Nature Reviews Physics 8, 146–159. arXiv:2510.05253 Most recent comprehensive review. [doi:10.1038/s42254-025-00916-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42254-025-00916-3)
- ⭐ **Torres, Blevins, Bassett & Eliassi-Rad (2021)** — *The why, how, and when of representations for complex systems.* SIAM Review. When and why different representations are appropriate. [doi:10.1137/20m1355896](https://doi.org/10.1137/20m1355896)
- ⭐ **Bianconi (2021)** — *Higher-Order Networks: An Introduction to Simplicial Complexes.* Cambridge. First dedicated textbook. Algebraic topology, Hodge theory, higher-order dynamics. Essential for higher-order network theory. [doi:10.1017/9781108770996](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108770996)
- ⭐ **Bick, Gross, Harrington & Schaub (2023)** — *What are higher-order networks?* SIAM Review. When higher-order representations are justified vs. modelling choices. [doi:10.1137/21m1414024](https://doi.org/10.1137/21m1414024)
