# Higher-Order Structures

The mathematical core shared by within- and across-agent work. Tier 1:
Battiston 2020 (the manifesto), Bianconi 2021 (the textbook). Tier 2: the rest.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Battiston, Cencetti, Iacopini, Latora, Lucas, Patania, Young & Petri (2020)** — *Networks beyond pairwise interactions: structure and dynamics.* Physics Reports. The manifesto. Defines the field.
- **Battiston et al. (2021)** — *The physics of higher-order interactions in complex systems.* Nature Physics. Perspective piece.
- **Battiston, Bick, Lucas, Millán, Skardal & Zhang (2026)** — *Collective dynamics on higher-order networks.* Nature Reviews Physics 8, 146–159. arXiv:2510.05253 Most recent comprehensive review.
- ⭐ **Torres, Blevins, Bassett & Eliassi-Rad (2021)** — *The why, how, and when of representations for complex systems.* SIAM Review. When and why different representations are appropriate.
- ⭐ **Bianconi (2021)** — *Higher-Order Networks: An Introduction to Simplicial Complexes.* Cambridge. First dedicated textbook. Algebraic topology, Hodge theory, higher-order dynamics. Essential for higher-order network theory.
- ⭐ **Bick, Gross, Harrington & Schaub (2023)** — *What are higher-order networks?* SIAM Review. When higher-order representations are justified vs. modelling choices.
