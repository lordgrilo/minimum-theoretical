# Time Series, Sequences & Dynamical Inference

Much NPLab data are sequences — fMRI, EEG, whale codas, social dynamics, and neural
nets during training. This is the shared toolkit for analysing them: stationarity,
state-space and hidden-Markov models, changepoints, directed information, and
surrogate-data null tests.

## Reading

- **Rabiner (1989)** — *A tutorial on hidden Markov models and selected applications in speech recognition.* Proceedings of the IEEE. HMMs for sequences — directly relevant to coda structure. [doi:10.1109/5.18626](https://doi.org/10.1109/5.18626)
- ⭐ **Shumway & Stoffer (2017)** — *Time Series Analysis and Its Applications* (4th ed.). Springer. AR / state-space models and spectral analysis. The standard. [doi:10.1007/978-3-319-52452-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-52452-8)
- **Schreiber (2000)** — *Measuring information transfer.* Physical Review Letters. Transfer entropy — model-free directed information. [doi:10.1103/physrevlett.85.461](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.85.461)
- ⭐ **Schreiber & Schmitz (2000)** — *Surrogate time series.* Physica D. Surrogate-data null tests for nonlinearity and coupling. [doi:10.1016/s0167-2789(00)00043-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0167-2789%2800%2900043-9)
- **Truong, Oudre & Vayatis (2020)** — *Selective review of offline change point detection methods.* Signal Processing. Segmenting nonstationary series. [doi:10.1016/j.sigpro.2019.107299](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sigpro.2019.107299)
