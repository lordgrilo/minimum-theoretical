# Time Series, Sequences & Dynamical Inference

Much NPLab data are sequences — fMRI, EEG, whale codas, social dynamics, and
neural nets during training. This is the shared toolkit for analysing them:
stationarity, state-space and hidden-Markov models, changepoints, directed
information, and surrogate-data null tests — the common language between the
CETI and neural-HOI work.

## Reading

- **Rabiner (1989)** — *A tutorial on hidden Markov models and selected applications in speech recognition.* Proceedings of the IEEE. HMMs for sequences — directly relevant to coda structure.
- ⭐ **Shumway & Stoffer (2017)** — *Time Series Analysis and Its Applications* (4th ed.). Springer. AR / state-space models and spectral analysis. The standard.
- **Schreiber (2000)** — *Measuring information transfer.* Physical Review Letters. Transfer entropy — model-free directed information.
- ⭐ **Schreiber & Schmitz (2000)** — *Surrogate time series.* Physica D. Surrogate-data null tests for nonlinearity and coupling.
- **Truong, Oudre & Vayatis (2020)** — *Selective review of offline change point detection methods.* Signal Processing. Segmenting nonstationary series.
