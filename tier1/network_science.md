# Network Science

The minimum vocabulary of graphs, scale-free and small-world structure, the
configuration model, spectra, and community detection as inference. Processes,
robustness and epidemics live in Tier 2 (Network Processes, Inference & Robustness).

## Reading

- ⭐ **Latora, Nicosia & Russo (2017)** — *Complex Networks: Principles, Methods and Applications.* Cambridge. Best modern textbook. Priority: Ch. 1–6, 10. [doi:10.1017/9781316216002](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781316216002)
- **Newman (2003)** — *The structure and function of complex networks.* SIAM Review. The survey that defined the field. [doi:10.1137/s003614450342480](https://doi.org/10.1137/s003614450342480)
- **Newman (2018)** — *Networks.* Oxford University Press. Full textbook. Reference for spectral methods, community detection, epidemics. [doi:10.1093/oso/9780198805090.001.0001](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780198805090.001.0001)
- **Watts & Strogatz (1998)** — *Collective dynamics of 'small-world' networks.* Nature. The small-world model. Short and elegant. [doi:10.1038/30918](https://doi.org/10.1038/30918)
- **Barabási & Albert (1999)** — *Emergence of scaling in random networks.* Science. Preferential attachment and scale-free networks. [doi:10.1126/science.286.5439.509](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.286.5439.509)
- **Newman, Strogatz & Watts (2001)** — *Random graphs with arbitrary degree distributions and their applications.* Physical Review E. The configuration model; degree distributions and epidemic thresholds. [doi:10.1103/physreve.64.026118](https://doi.org/10.1103/physreve.64.026118)
- **Chung (1997)** — *Spectral Graph Theory.* CBMS / AMS. Foundational Laplacian spectral methods — grounding for Hodge Laplacians and Laplacian renormalization. [doi:10.1090/cbms/092](https://doi.org/10.1090/cbms/092)
- **Newman (2006)** — *Modularity and community structure in networks.* PNAS. The modularity matrix and spectral community detection. [doi:10.1073/pnas.0601602103](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0601602103)
- ⭐ **Peixoto (2023)** — *Descriptive vs. inferential community detection in networks.* Cambridge Elements. The principled network-inference position statement. [doi:10.1017/9781009118897](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009118897)
- **Delvenne, Yaliraki & Barahona (2010)** — *Stability of graph communities across time scales.* PNAS 107, 12755–12760. Markov stability: a partition read as a time scale of a diffusion process. The bridge from graph operators to coarse-graining, and a check on choosing the preserved quantity after the fact. [doi:10.1073/pnas.0903215107](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0903215107)
