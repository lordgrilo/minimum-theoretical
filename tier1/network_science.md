# Network Science

The substrate of all across-agent work — the minimum vocabulary of graphs,
scale-free and small-world structure, the configuration model, spectra, and
community detection as inference. Processes, robustness and epidemics live in
Tier 2 (Network Processes, Inference & Robustness).

## Reading

- ⭐ **Latora, Nicosia & Russo (2017)** — *Complex Networks: Principles, Methods and Applications.* Cambridge. Best modern textbook. Priority: Ch. 1–6, 10.
- **Newman (2003)** — *The structure and function of complex networks.* SIAM Review. The survey that defined the field.
- **Newman (2018)** — *Networks.* Oxford University Press. Full textbook. Reference for spectral methods, community detection, epidemics.
- **Watts & Strogatz (1998)** — *Collective dynamics of 'small-world' networks.* Nature. The small-world model. Short and elegant.
- **Barabási & Albert (1999)** — *Emergence of scaling in random networks.* Science. Preferential attachment and scale-free networks.
- **Newman, Strogatz & Watts (2001)** — *Random graphs with arbitrary degree distributions and their applications.* Physical Review E. The configuration model; degree distributions and epidemic thresholds.
- **Chung (1997)** — *Spectral Graph Theory.* CBMS / AMS. Foundational Laplacian spectral methods — grounding for Hodge Laplacians and Laplacian renormalization.
- **Newman (2006)** — *Modularity and community structure in networks.* PNAS. The modularity matrix and spectral community detection.
- ⭐ **Peixoto (2023)** — *Descriptive vs. inferential community detection in networks.* Cambridge Elements. The principled network-inference position statement.
