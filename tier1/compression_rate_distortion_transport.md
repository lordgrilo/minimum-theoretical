# Compression, Rate–Distortion & Optimal Transport

In this lab, information theory means compression under task constraints — not
just entropy and mutual information. Rate–distortion, the information bottleneck
and optimal transport are the shared machinery behind semantic horizons, VLM
geometry, efficient coding and animal communication. (See also Cover & Thomas
Ch. 10 and Tishby's Information Bottleneck under Information Theory.)

## Reading

- ⭐ **Peyré & Cuturi (2019)** — *Computational Optimal Transport.* Foundations and Trends in ML. The practical OT reference: algorithms, Sinkhorn, applications. Start here. [doi:10.1561/9781680835519](https://doi.org/10.1561/9781680835519)
- **Shannon (1959)** — *Coding theorems for a discrete source with a fidelity criterion.* IRE National Convention Record, Part 4 (repr. in *Collected Papers*, IEEE Press). The origin of rate–distortion theory. [doi:10.1109/9780470544242.ch21](https://doi.org/10.1109/9780470544242.ch21)
- **Berger (1971)** — *Rate Distortion Theory: A Mathematical Basis for Data Compression.* Prentice-Hall. The classic monograph; R(D) in depth. [link](https://books.google.com/books?vid=ISBN9780137531035)
- **Cuturi (2013)** — *Sinkhorn distances: lightspeed computation of optimal transport.* NeurIPS. Entropic regularization; made OT computationally practical. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.1306.0895](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1306.0895) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.0895)
- **Villani (2009)** — *Optimal Transport: Old and New.* Springer. Comprehensive mathematical reference (Ch. 1–6). [doi:10.1007/978-3-540-71050-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-71050-9)
