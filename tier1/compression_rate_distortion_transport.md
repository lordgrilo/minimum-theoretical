# Compression, Rate–Distortion & Optimal Transport

In this lab, information theory means compression under task constraints — not
just entropy and mutual information. Rate–distortion, the information bottleneck
and optimal transport are the shared machinery behind semantic horizons, VLM
geometry, efficient coding and animal communication. (See also Cover & Thomas
Ch. 10 and Tishby's Information Bottleneck under Information Theory.)

## Reading

- ⭐ **Peyré & Cuturi (2019)** — *Computational Optimal Transport.* Foundations and Trends in ML. The practical OT reference: algorithms, Sinkhorn, applications. Start here.
- **Shannon (1959)** — *Coding theorems for a discrete source with a fidelity criterion.* IRE National Convention Record. The origin of rate–distortion theory.
- **Berger (1971)** — *Rate Distortion Theory: A Mathematical Basis for Data Compression.* Prentice-Hall. The classic monograph; R(D) in depth.
- **Cuturi (2013)** — *Sinkhorn distances: lightspeed computation of optimal transport.* NeurIPS. Entropic regularization; made OT computationally practical.
- **Villani (2008)** — *Optimal Transport: Old and New.* Springer. Comprehensive mathematical reference (Ch. 1–6).
