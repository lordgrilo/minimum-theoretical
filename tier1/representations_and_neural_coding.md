# Representations & Neural Coding

Work on semanticity and on vision–language models rests on a theory of how finite
systems *represent* — efficient coding, the geometry of population codes, and how
representations are compared. Read these before any stream about brains, minds or
models.

## Reading

- **Barlow (1961)** — *Possible principles underlying the transformations of sensory messages.* In Sensory Communication (MIT Press). The efficient-coding hypothesis: sensory systems minimise redundancy. Foundational for work on representational limits. [doi:10.7551/mitpress/9780262518420.003.0013](https://doi.org/10.7551/mitpress/9780262518420.003.0013)
- **Dayan & Abbott (2001)** — *Theoretical Neuroscience.* MIT Press. Gold-standard computational neuroscience text. Ch. 1–4: neural coding, information in spike trains, population codes. [link](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262541855/theoretical-neuroscience/)
- **Rieke, Warland, de Ruyter van Steveninck & Bialek (1997)** — *Spikes: Exploring the Neural Code.* MIT Press. Neural coding and information theory applied to spike trains. [link](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262681087/spikes/)
- ⭐ **Kriegeskorte, Mur & Bandettini (2008)** — *Representational similarity analysis.* Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience. The method for comparing representations across brains and models via dissimilarity matrices. [doi:10.3389/neuro.06.004.2008](https://doi.org/10.3389/neuro.06.004.2008)
- **Chung, Lee & Sompolinsky (2018)** — *Classification and geometry of general perceptual manifolds.* Physical Review X. Manifold capacity: links representation geometry to what a system can classify. [doi:10.1103/physrevx.8.031003](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevx.8.031003)
- **Bernardi, Benna, Rigotti, Munuera, Fusi & Salzman (2020)** — *The geometry of abstraction in the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex.* Cell. Abstract variables encoded as geometric configurations balancing generalization and shattering dimensionality. [doi:10.1016/j.cell.2020.09.031](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.09.031)
- **Vyas, Golub, Sussillo & Shenoy (2020)** — *Computation through neural population dynamics.* Annual Review of Neuroscience. The neural-manifold framework review; connects population geometry to computation. [doi:10.1146/annurev-neuro-092619-094115](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-neuro-092619-094115)
