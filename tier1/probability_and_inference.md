# Probability, Statistics & Bayesian Inference

How we reason under uncertainty and infer structure from data. Tier 1: Jaynes
Ch. 1–4, Gelman BDA. Tier 2: Bishop, Peixoto.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Jaynes (2003)** — *Probability Theory: The Logic of Science.* Cambridge. Philosophical foundation for Bayesian reasoning. Ch. 1–4, 11.
- ⭐ **Gelman, Carlin, Stern, Dunson, Vehtari & Rubin (2013)** — *Bayesian Data Analysis* (3rd ed.). CRC. The practical reference. Hierarchical models, MCMC, model checking.
- **Bishop (2006)** — *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning.* Springer. Ch. 1–2, 8, 10. ML perspective on Bayesian methods.
- **Andrieu, De Freitas, Doucet & Jordan (2003)** — *An introduction to MCMC for machine learning.* Machine Learning. Standard MCMC tutorial.
- **Peixoto (2019)** — *Bayesian stochastic blockmodel inference* (papers + graph-tool). State-of-the-art principled network inference.
