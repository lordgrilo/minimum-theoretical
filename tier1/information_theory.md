# Information Theory

The common language. Tier 1: Cover & Thomas Ch. 2, 4, 10. Tier 2: MacKay.
Tier 3: the Information Bottleneck. We also trace the *multivariate* lineage
(total correlation, interaction information) that becomes O-information / PID.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Cover & Thomas (2006)** — *Elements of Information Theory.* Wiley. THE textbook. Priority: Ch. 2 (entropy), 4 (MI), 10 (rate-distortion). [doi:10.1002/047174882x](https://doi.org/10.1002/047174882x)
- **MacKay (2003)** — *Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms.* Cambridge. More intuitive than Cover & Thomas. Freely available online. [link](http://www.inference.org.uk/mackay/itila/)
- ⭐ **Tishby, Pereira & Bialek (1999)** — *The Information Bottleneck Method.* Proc. 37th Allerton Conference on Communication, Control and Computing, 368–377. arXiv:physics/0004057. Compression–relevance tradeoff. Theoretical ancestor of the semanticity framework. [doi:10.48550/arXiv.physics/0004057](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.physics/0004057) [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0004057)
- ⭐ **Zaslavsky, Kemp, Regier & Tishby (2018)** — *Efficient compression in color naming and its evolution.* PNAS. IB explains cross-linguistic color-naming universals. Beautiful empirical validation. [doi:10.1073/pnas.1800521115](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1800521115)
- ⭐ **Watanabe (1960)** — *Information theoretical analysis of multivariate correlation.* IBM Journal of Research and Development. Introduces total correlation (multi-information) — the conceptual root of multivariate dependency measures. [doi:10.1147/rd.41.0066](https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.41.0066)
- **McGill (1954)** — *Multivariate information transmission.* Psychometrika. Interaction information; documents the negativity issue that later motivated PID and O-information. [doi:10.1007/bf02289159](https://doi.org/10.1007/bf02289159)
