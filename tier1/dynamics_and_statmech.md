# Dynamical Systems & Statistical Mechanics

The physics underneath emergence and universality. Tier 1: Strogatz Ch. 1–8,
Sethna Ch. 2/7/8/12. Tier 2: Kuramoto -> higher-order networks; social dynamics.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Strogatz (2015)** — *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos* (2nd ed.). Westview Press; reissued by CRC Press, 2018. Best introductory text. Ch. 1–8. [doi:10.1201/9780429492563](https://doi.org/10.1201/9780429492563)
- ⭐ **Sethna (2006)** — *Statistical Mechanics: Entropy, Order Parameters, and Complexity.* Oxford. Best modern stat-mech for complex systems. Freely available. Ch. 2, 7, 8, 12. [link](https://sethna.lassp.cornell.edu/StatMech/)
- **Pikovsky, Rosenblum & Kurths (2001)** — *Synchronization: A Universal Concept in Nonlinear Sciences.* Cambridge. The reference for synchronization phenomena. [doi:10.1017/cbo9780511755743](https://doi.org/10.1017/cbo9780511755743)
- ⭐ **Wilson (1975)** — *The renormalization group: critical phenomena and the Kondo problem.* Reviews of Modern Physics. The foundational RG paper. (Also Tier 3: Renormalization.) [doi:10.1103/revmodphys.47.773](https://doi.org/10.1103/revmodphys.47.773)
