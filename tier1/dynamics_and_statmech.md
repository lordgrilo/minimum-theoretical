# Dynamical Systems & Statistical Mechanics

The physics underneath emergence and universality. Tier 1: Strogatz Ch. 1–8,
Sethna Ch. 2/7/8/12. Tier 2: Kuramoto -> higher-order networks; social dynamics.

## Reading

- ⭐ **Strogatz (2015)** — *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos* (2nd ed.). Westview Press (verify publisher of record). Best introductory text. Ch. 1–8.
- ⭐ **Sethna (2006)** — *Statistical Mechanics: Entropy, Order Parameters, and Complexity.* Oxford. Best modern stat-mech for complex systems. Freely available. Ch. 2, 7, 8, 12.
- **Pikovsky, Rosenblum & Kurths (2001)** — *Synchronization: A Universal Concept in Nonlinear Sciences.* Cambridge. The reference for synchronization phenomena.
- ⭐ **Wilson (1975)** — *The renormalization group: critical phenomena and the Kondo problem.* Reviews of Modern Physics. The foundational RG paper. (Also Tier 3: Renormalization.)
